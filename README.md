PROMPT

You are an elite senior software architect, UI/UX designer, product designer, database architect, and full-stack engineer.

Build a world-class SaaS platform called Cardix (temporary name).

The application must focus ONLY on Digital Business Cards.

Do NOT turn the project into LinkedIn, CRM, Social Network, Marketplace, Booking Platform, or Employee Management System.

Everything in the application must revolve around creating, managing, sharing, scanning, and saving digital business cards.

The application should feel like an Apple-quality product with a premium minimal design.

PROJECT GOAL

Create the most modern digital business card platform in the world.

The platform should allow users to:

• Create beautiful digital business cards.

• Share them instantly.

• Save business cards received from others.

• Scan paper business cards using AI OCR.

• Share cards using QR Code.

• Share cards using NFC.

• Share cards using a personal URL.

• Synchronize cards across devices.

Nothing outside this scope.
